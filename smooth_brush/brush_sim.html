Opacity is the alpha of a single dab, the same for both models. The only difference is storage precision:
8-bit: each dab is quantised to 8 bits and blended into the layer 8-bit over 8-bit.
IllustStudio: dabs accumulate in a 16-bit buffer (1.0 = 32768), and the layer pixel is recomputed every time from a snapshot of the layer taken at stroke start: dst = orig + round((255−orig)·acc/32768).
For low values, turn on gain ×16 and contours.
